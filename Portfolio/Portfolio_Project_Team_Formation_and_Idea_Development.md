### **Brainstorming**

#### **What Is a Network Assessment, Automated Network Monitoring Dashboard, or Basic Vulnerability Scanner — and Why It Matters Right Now**

A **network assessment** is a structured evaluation of an organization’s IT infrastructure — covering performance, security posture, device inventory, and compliance readiness — to give IT leaders a clear, current picture of what’s actually running on their network.

**At a glance:**
- **What it is:** A comprehensive review of hardware, software, configurations, and security controls  
- **What it finds:** Vulnerabilities, performance bottlenecks, compliance gaps, undocumented or outdated assets  
- **Who needs it:** Any organization running critical infrastructure — especially regulated industries  
- **How often:** At minimum annually, or after major IT changes  
- **What you get:** Prioritized remediation plan, executive summary, and a baseline for ongoing monitoring

## **Summary of Selected MVP**

A **Network Assessment MVP** that provides a structured, repeatable process to evaluate any network. It includes inventory, security checks, performance metrics, configuration review, and risk analysis. Its impact lies in improving reliability, security, and documentation.

Most IT leaders assume they know what’s on their network. Many don’t — at least not fully.

Devices get added. Configurations drift. Former employees leave accounts active. Vendors make undocumented changes. Over time, the gap between what you *think* your network looks like and what it *actually* looks like grows.

This gap leads to:
- Security vulnerabilities  
- Compliance oversights  
- Performance issues  

Industry research shows network downtime can cost **$336,000 per hour** for many organizations. Often, the most impactful issues are foundational: an unpatched device, an open management interface, or active credentials for a former employee.

Modern networks are complex — on‑prem, cloud, hybrid, remote endpoints, IoT. Maintaining an accurate picture is a real operational challenge.

A **network assessment** is designed to solve exactly that.
